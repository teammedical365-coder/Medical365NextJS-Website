Core Capabilities

Everything you need, purpose-built for your workflow.

Vitals & Observation Charting

Quick entry for temperature, pulse, BP, SpO2, and custom parameters. Abnormal values auto-flagged to the duty doctor.

Medication Administration Record

Scan-and-administer workflow with drug, dose, route, and time verification. Prevents wrong patient and wrong medication errors.

Shift Handover Notes

Structured handover templates ensure no patient information is lost between shifts. Digital sign-off by oncoming nurse.

Nursing Care Plans

Evidence-based care plan templates aligned with NANDA nursing diagnoses. Track interventions and evaluate outcomes.

Task & Assignment Management

Charge nurse dashboard to assign tasks, track completion, and balance workload across the nursing team.

Patient Safety Alerts

Auto-alerts for fall risk, pressure ulcer risk, DVT prophylaxis, and allergy warnings. Closes the loop on safety protocols.
