Core Capabilities

Everything you need, purpose-built for your workflow.

Live Bed Status Dashboard

Colour-coded real-time map of all beds by ward, floor, and specialty. Occupied, vacant, cleaning, reserved — all at a glance.

Smart Bed Allocation

Auto-suggest the best bed based on specialty, patient gender, isolation requirements, and care level. Allocate in under 60 seconds.

Housekeeping Workflow

Discharge triggers auto-housekeeping task. Bed marked "cleaning" until housekeeper marks complete. Track turnaround time.

Waitlist Management

Automatically queue waitlisted patients and notify them the moment a bed in their required ward becomes available.

Bed Occupancy Analytics

Occupancy trends by ward, specialty, and time period. Identify bottlenecks and optimize bed allocation policies with data.

Multi-Facility View

For hospital chains, view bed availability across all facilities in one dashboard. Transfer patients to the nearest available bed.
