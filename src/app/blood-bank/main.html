Core Capabilities

Everything you need, purpose-built for your workflow.

Donor Registration & Screening

Complete donor history, eligibility screening (TTI, medical history), and donor card issuance. Deferred donor tracking.

Component Preparation & Labeling

Blood component separation (RBC, FFP, Platelets, Cryoprecipitate) with barcode labeling and ISBT 128 compliance.

Cross-Matching & Compatibility

ABO/Rh typing, antibody screening, and compatibility testing. Results recorded digitally with technician sign-off.

Inventory & Expiry Management

FIFO-based inventory management. Expiry alerts, near-expiry utilization prompts, and wastage tracking.

Transfusion Records

Complete transfusion documentation including issue, administration, patient reaction monitoring, and post-transfusion notes.

NBTC Compliance Reports

Auto-generate reports required by the National Blood Transfusion Council. Donor deferral registers, issue logs, and adverse reaction reports.
