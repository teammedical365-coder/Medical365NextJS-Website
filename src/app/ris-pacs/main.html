Core Capabilities

Everything you need, purpose-built for your workflow.

Radiology Order Management

Orders from EMR auto-populate in the RIS worklist. Priority flagging, patient prep instructions, and slot management.

DICOM Viewer (PACS)

Web-based zero-download DICOM viewer. Multi-series display, measurement tools, windowing, and comparison views.

Structured Reporting

Template-based radiology reports with critical finding integration. Voice dictation support and spell-check with medical vocabulary.

Critical Finding Alerts

Immediate notification to referring physician for critical/urgent findings via in-app alert, SMS, and call escalation.

Teleradiology

Remote reporting for after-hours coverage. Studies routed to off-site radiologists with DICOM quality preservation.

Patient Image Sharing

Patients receive a secure link to their DICOM images and report. Download or share with any specialist globally.
