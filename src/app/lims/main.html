Core Capabilities

Everything you need, purpose-built for your workflow.

Medical365 LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System) supports bidirectional integration with major lab analyzers, enabling auto-upload of test results directly into patient EMR records. With customizable report templates, QC tracking, and ABDM-compliant data handling, our LIMS eliminates manual transcription errors and speeds up diagnostic turnaround for hospitals and diagnostic centers across India.

Sample Collection & Barcode Tracking

Barcode and QR labels generated at order. Track every sample from phlebotomy to analyzer to result — zero mix-ups.

Analyzer Integration

Bidirectional interface with 50+ lab analyzers (Roche, Abbott, Siemens, Mindray). Results imported automatically, no manual entry.

Critical Value Alerts

Automatically notifies the ordering physician via in-app alert and SMS when a critical value is detected. Full audit trail.

Reference Range Management

Age, gender, and population-specific reference ranges. Abnormal flags auto-applied on result entry.

TAT Monitoring

Real-time tracking of turnaround time by test type, shift, and technician. SLA breach alerts for urgent tests.

Patient Result Portal

Patients access lab results via portal and WhatsApp PDF as soon as validated. Reduces counter traffic by 60%.
